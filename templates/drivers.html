Key Takeaway

Primary Drivers: Specialty drugs and healthcare utilization are the leading sources of medical cost pressure, making pharmacy management and acute-care utilization key areas for intervention.
Secondary Pressure: Site-of-care patterns and provider mix contribute additional cost escalation, particularly where higher-cost inpatient or specialist services are used more frequently.
Offset Opportunity: Greater adoption of generic and biosimilar medicines can help offset pharmacy-related cost growth and improve overall cost efficiency.
Recommended Focus: Prioritize specialty-drug optimization, avoidable ER and inpatient utilization reduction, site-of-care management, and provider-network optimization to contain future medical cost escalation.
